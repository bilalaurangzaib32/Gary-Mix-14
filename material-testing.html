AIRBUS AIMS 09-00-002

AIRCRAFT INTERIOR CLEANING

Water-based cleaners for interior hard surfaces

Evaluation of water-based cleaning products for use on aircraft interior structures and surfaces.

AIRBUS AIMS 09-00-003 (Issue 5)

INTERIOR DISINFECTION

Disinfectants on aircraft interior surfaces

Assessment of disinfectant compatibility with aircraft interior materials under the referenced issue status.

AIRBUS AMS 1452 / 1453A

AIRCRAFT SURFACE DISINFECTION

Disinfectants on aircraft surfaces

Method-based testing covering disinfectant contact with surfaces.

BOEING BSS7434

CHEMICAL COMPATIBILITY

Cleaning products on interior components

Method-based evaluation of specific cleaning products with aircraft interior materials and parts or drainage material.

ASTM F483 - 09

CORROSION

Immersion corrosion test

Immersion corrosion testing for aircraft maintenance chemicals on metallic substrates.

ASTM F484 - 08

ACRYLIC COMPATIBILITY

Stress cracking on acrylic surfaces

Evaluation of the effect of aircraft maintenance chemicals on stressed, non-stressed, or crazing-prone acrylics.

ASTM F485 - 08

SURFACE EFFECTS

Effects on unpainted surfaces

Assesses the effect of aircraft maintenance chemicals on unpainted metallic surfaces.

ASTM F1110 - 07

COLOUR CHANGE

Cleaning-fluid colour change on hard surfaces

Determines colour change caused by cleaning fluids on treated or coated aerospace surfaces.
